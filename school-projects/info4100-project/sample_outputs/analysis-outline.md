# Analysis outline (INFO 4100 — public summary)

This file summarizes **what the report covers** without reproducing raw student-level records or proprietary systems.

## Questions addressed

1. How do **enrollment counts** vary by term and course level over the study window?
2. Which **progression patterns** (e.g., sequencing into upper-level courses) appear most common in the cohorts studied?
3. What do **statistical models** suggest about drivers of enrollment or completion-style outcomes in the de-identified dataset?

## Workflow (mirrors the report structure)

| Stage | Activities |
|-------|------------|
| **Define cohorts** | Matriculation or entry terms; inclusion rules stated in the report |
| **Explore** | Summary tables and plots of enrollment and pathway counts |
| **Model** | R-based regression / trend models (specifications in the report) |
| **Interpret** | Plain-language takeaways for advisors or program review |
